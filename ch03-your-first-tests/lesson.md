# 3. Your First Tests: Totals

Chapter 2 showed what a test claims. This chapter writes some. You will take the three numbers you worked out by hand for INV-1002, the subtotal, the tax and the total, and write each one into a test. Every test is arranged, acted on and asserted, in that order.

The chapter has three steps and a summary. You will read a test with its three sections marked, fill in the expected values for three tests from your own calculations, and then see what goes wrong when an expected value is copied from the program. The library changes in only one step, and only in a copy.
